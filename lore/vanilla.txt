SETTING: this game starts in 1936 from the world as it really stood, and from there it is open: any country may take another government, other friends and enemies, other wars. What happened in real history after that is not a script; what happens here is decided in play. Trust the live situation below over your own historical knowledge, and let each country act on its present rulers' outlook and interests, not on the part history gave it.
